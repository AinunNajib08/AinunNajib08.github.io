About the App

GPS Map Camera: Geotag Photo helps field workers, engineers, land surveyors, and travelers capture photos and videos stamped with real-time GPS coordinates, altitude, compass orientation, clean minimap snapshots, and address telemetry.

Privacy Policy

Nadirah Labs values your privacy. GPS Map Camera processes location and camera data locally on your device to embed coordinates onto captured photos and videos. We do not sell or track your personal location data.

  • Camera & Microphone: Used exclusively for capturing photos and videos within the application.
  • Location: Used strictly to fetch GPS coordinates, address, and minimaps for media timestamps.
  • Storage: Used to save captured media directly to your device storage and gallery.
